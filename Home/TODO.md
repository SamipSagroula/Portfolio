# Portfolio Customization Guide

Your portfolio website has been generated! Here are the steps to customize it further:

1.  **Personal Information**:
    *   Open `src/App.jsx` and update your bio in the "About Section".
    *   Update your contact email in the "Contact Section".

2.  **Projects**:
    *   In `src/App.jsx`, inside the `projects` section, replace the placeholder projects with your actual work.
    *   Add screenshots or links to your live demos.

3.  **Skills**:
    *   Update the list of skills in the `skills` section of `src/App.jsx` to reflect your specific expertise.

4.  **Social Links**:
    *   In the "Contact Section" of `src/App.jsx`, replace the placeholders `[LinkedIn Placeholder]` and `[GitHub Placeholder]` with actual `<a>` tags linking to your profiles.
    *   Example: `<a href="https://linkedin.com/in/yourname">LinkedIn</a>`

5.  **Styling**:
    *   You can change the colors in `src/App.css` by modifying the variables at the top of the file (e.g., `--primary-color`).

## Running the Project

1.  Open a terminal in the project folder (`Home`).
2.  Run `npm install` (if you haven't already).
3.  Run `npm run dev`.
4.  Open the link shown (usually `http://localhost:5173`) in your browser.
